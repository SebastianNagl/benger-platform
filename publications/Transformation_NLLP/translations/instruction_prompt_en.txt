Follow the rules below.

1. EXAMINATION MANDATE AND SCOPE

- First determine the concrete task instruction and all candidate instructions (Bearbeitervermerke).
- The task instruction and the candidate instructions bindingly determine which questions are to be addressed.
- Whatever is expressly excluded from examination must not receive a scored step.
- Where task instruction, candidate instructions, and model solution diverge, the task instruction and candidate instructions prevail as regards the scope of examination.
- Briefly summarize the recognized examination mandate and the candidate instructions in the "meta" field.
- If the examination mandate is ambiguous, contradictory, or incomplete, note this under "warnungen" and proceed with the professionally most plausible interpretation.

2. RELATIONSHIP BETWEEN MODEL SOLUTION AND OWN LEGAL KNOWLEDGE

- The model solution is the primary, but not the exclusive, basis of the grading scheme.
- Adopt from it in particular:
  a) the intended examination structure,
  b) the bases of claims, statutory elements, and legal questions addressed,
  c) the case-specific arguments,
  d) the recognizable focal problems of the exam, and
  e) the expected results.

- Additionally use your own legal knowledge where this is necessary to:
  a) make gradable an examination performance contained only implicitly in the model solution,
  b) supplement an intermediate step necessary for the legal derivation,
  c) include a professionally necessary core content that the model solution recognizably omits,
  d) take into account a fact-proximate defensible opposing view, or
  e) map an alternative, legally defensible examination path.

- Externally supplemented content is admissible only if it:
  a) is opened up by the concrete facts of the case,
  b) is compatible with the task instruction and candidate instructions,
  c) is relevant for professionally sound or fair grading, and
  d) rests on a recognized legal view or a methodically comprehensible legal argumentation.

- Not to be included are:
  a) merely abstractly conceivable or far-fetched problems,
  b) additional problems without influence on the concrete solution of the case,
  c) variants that presuppose facts not provided,
  d) merely theoretically interesting doctrinal disputes without relevance to the case,
  e) unsubstantiated divergent results, and
  f) solutions that disregard unambiguous requirements of statute, case facts, or candidate instructions.

- Externally supplemented content that every complete solution must address may be included as a regular grading step.
- Content that becomes necessary only under a specific defensible solution path belongs in "alternative_loesungswege" and not as an additional regular step in the main scheme.

3. HANDLING PROBLEMS OF THE MODEL SOLUTION

- Do not silently adopt a recognizably problematic determination of the model solution.
- If the model solution's solution path is professionally defensible, map it as the primary path and, where appropriate, supplement further defensible solutions.
- If a determination of the model solution is not defensible by legal standards, flag this under "warnungen" and align the grading with a defensible solution.
- Justify the deviation concretely, without inventing sources or authorities that were not provided.
- If the state of the law is not specified and a supplement possibly depends on legal rules that change over time, flag this uncertainty under "warnungen".

4. FOCAL PROBLEMS OF THE EXAM (KLAUSURSCHWERPUNKTE)

- Determine the legally central and argumentatively demanding problem complexes of the concrete task.
- The number of focal problems follows from the material. Produce neither a minimum number nor a fixed quota.
- Assess focal-problem quality in particular by:
  a) the significance for the concrete task instruction,
  b) the legal difficulty,
  c) the required depth of interpretation, balancing, or argumentation,
  d) the required case-specific subsumption, and
  e) the effects on the further solution path.

- Do not claim that a problem empirically separates good from weak submissions. Such a statement is impossible without submission data.
- Mark membership in a focal problem exclusively via the "schwerpunkt_id" field of the regular steps concerned; the objects under "klausurschwerpunkte" contain no step lists and no points.
- Each regular step may be assigned to at most one focal problem.
- The point total of a focal problem is computed by machine from its assigned steps; do not state it.

5. FORMING THE GRADING STEPS

- Order the grading steps along the professionally appropriate examination structure.
- Sections and examination stations serve structuring purposes but receive no points independent of their steps.
- A step is a legal examination performance gradable independently in substance, which can be delivered separately as correct, partially correct, or incorrect.
- Separate two performances if one can be delivered correctly or incorrectly independently of the other.
- Merge performances if they are regularly graded on the basis of the same student passages and do not constitute meaningfully separable partial performances.
- A step may constitute neither a complete extensive examination station nor merely a heading, a bare result, or a single interchangeable sentence.
- Avoid both needless one-point atomization and steps so broad that substantially different partial performances can no longer be graded differentially.
- Definition and subsumption are not automatically separate steps. Separate them only if, in the concrete case, they constitute independently gradable performances.
- Recognizing a basis of claim, a norm, or a problem may be scored independently, and with low weight, only if that recognition substantively steers the further solution path.
- Not every heading of the model solution must produce its own grading step.
- No step may score the same student performance that another step already scores.
- Bare intermediate or final results receive no points of their own. The correctness of the result is taken into account within the associated substantive step.
- Use globally unique and consecutive IDs:
  Sections: A1, A2, ...
  Focal problems: SP1, SP2, ...
  Regular steps: S1, S2, ...
  Alternative solution paths: ALT1, ALT2, ...
  Steps within an alternative: ALT1-S1, ALT1-S2, ...

6. PROVENANCE AND TRACEABILITY

- Mark every regular step and every alternative solution path with exactly one provenance:
  "musterloesung_explizit",
  "musterloesung_implizit", or
  "extern_ergaenzt".

- "musterloesung_explizit" means the graded performance is expressly presented in the model solution.
- "musterloesung_implizit" means the performance is directly presupposed by the solution path presented there but is not independently spelled out.
- "extern_ergaenzt" means the performance or alternative is supplemented on the basis of your own legal knowledge.

- For "musterloesung_explizit" and "musterloesung_implizit", a reference must be given:
  a) a heading or unambiguous description of the position, and
  b) a text anchor as short as possible (guideline: up to 20 words).

- For "extern_ergaenzt", "fundstelle_musterloesung" remains null. Instead, "fachliche_begruendung_externe_ergaenzung" must concretely explain:
  a) why the supplement is legally necessary or defensible,
  b) how it connects to the case facts, and
  c) why it is relevant for grading.

- Do not invent sources, court decisions, literature opinions, or labels such as "prevailing opinion" if their existence is not certain.
- A doctrinal or methodological justification suffices. Concrete source citations are not required.

7. WEIGHTING

- State a relative weight as an integer for every regular step ("gewicht").
- Guideline: the weights of all regular steps should move in the order of magnitude of 100 in total. An exact sum is NOT required – the final point values are normalized by machine to exactly 100 raw points; the order and ratios of your weights are preserved, and every step receives at least one point.
- State no section points, no focal-problem points, and no point budgets – they are computed by machine.
- There is no free point pool, no bonus points, and no negative points.
- Alternative solution paths do not change the total of 100 points.

- Weight the steps in particular by:
  a) significance for the task instruction,
  b) legal difficulty,
  c) required subsumption performance,
  d) required depth of argumentation or balancing,
  e) effects on the further examination path, and
  f) the effort to be expected for a complete treatment.

- The length of the presentation in the model solution is only a subordinate indication.
- The mere number of norms or arguments mentioned is not an independent weighting criterion.
- Assign every regular step to exactly one weighting class:

  "routine":
  necessary, predominantly standardized examination or application;

  "tragend":
  independent examination essential for the concrete solution path;

  "schwerpunkt":
  central problem with increased depth of justification, balancing, interpretation, or subsumption.

- A focal step regularly receives clearly more weight than a comparable routine performance. However, no rigid numerical ratio applies.
- Briefly justify the concrete weighting of each step with reference to the case.

8. GRADING GUIDANCE AND PARTIAL CREDIT

- Formulate exclusively observable professional performance requirements.
- Avoid indeterminate empty formulas such as:
  "well reasoned",
  "essentially correct",
  "largely complete",
  "with minor errors", or
  "defensible exposition",
  unless it is concretely stated which performance is present and which is missing.

- "volle_punkte" describes, to the extent pertinent to the step:
  a) the required problem identification,
  b) the legal standard,
  c) the case-specific evaluation of the relevant facts,
  d) the required subsumption,
  e) the required arguments and counter-arguments, and
  f) a result derived from these in a methodically defensible way.

- "keine_punkte" concretely describes when no relevant performance for this step is present.
- A mere repetition of the case facts without legal processing receives no points.
- Merely naming a result receives no points if the graded performance consists in its legal justification.
- An incorrect final result does not automatically lead to zero points if relevant legal standards, subsumptions, or arguments were developed correctly.

- The "teilpunktstufen" are an ordered list of performance descriptions from the weakest to the strongest creditable partial performance – WITHOUT point boundaries; the point ranges are assigned by machine.
- Each level concretely describes which required performances are present and which essential performances are missing or defective.
- Use as few partial-credit levels as possible and as many as necessary; for weighty steps two to four levels regularly suffice, and for low-weighted steps the list may remain empty.
- Requirements must increase cumulatively from lower to higher levels.
- Avoid levels that differ only by words such as "partially", "predominantly", or "largely".

9. DEFENSIBLE ALTERNATIVE SOLUTION PATHS

- For legally open, interpretation-dependent, or disputed questions, actively check whether, besides the primary solution path, further fact-proximate and methodically defensible solutions exist.
- An alternative may be included even if it is not mentioned in the model solution.
- An alternative is defensible if:
  a) it connects to the facts as provided,
  b) it is compatible with the task instruction and candidate instructions,
  c) it rests on a recognized legal view or a methodically comprehensible interpretation,
  d) the required legal standards and case-specific arguments can be presented, and
  e) a professionally competent corrector could not grade it as wrong solely because of its divergent result.

- Merely asserting a different result is not a defensible alternative.
- Do not include an alternative solely because it is theoretically conceivable. It must come seriously into professional consideration in a real student submission.
- Assign each alternative to a type:

  "lokal":
  the alternative concerns a single legal question without substantially changing the subsequent examination structure.

  "strukturveraendernd":
  the alternative changes the further examination structure, causes regular steps to lapse, or requires different follow-up examinations.

- For each alternative describe:
  a) the legal question at which it starts,
  b) its connecting points in the case facts,
  c) its doctrinal or methodological basis,
  d) the arguments required for its defensibility,
  e) its effects on the further examination path, and
  f) its grading within the already existing point frame.

- A defensible alternative is graded by the quality of its own legal reasoning.
- It does not receive fewer points solely because it deviates from the result or structure of the model solution.
- Full points presuppose, on the alternative path too, a complete legal derivation, case-specific subsumption, and methodically comprehensible argumentation.

- Every alternative solution path must name, via "ersetzt_schritt_ids", the regular steps whose point frame it replaces.
- The point frame follows from these steps by machine; do not state it.
- State relative weights for the "alternative_schritte"; their point values are assigned by machine within the point frame.
- Alternative solution paths create no additional or bonus points.
- The regular and the alternative solution path must not be scored cumulatively for the same performance.
- Mutually exclusive alternatives must be marked under "nicht_kumulativ_mit".
- If a submission mixes contradictory solution paths, only partial performances actually delivered coherently may be scored, and no performance may be scored twice.

10. DEPENDENCIES AND CONSEQUENTIAL ERRORS

- List under "abhaengig_von" the IDs of earlier steps whose legal course-setting influences the entry into the step concerned.
- Do not penalize the same initial error more than once.
- A follow-up examination that was actually carried out and is consistent on its own premise is to be graded by its independent legal quality.
- An incorrect earlier course-setting does not automatically exclude partial or full points for a professionally correct follow-up examination.
- However, the consequential-error rule does not lead to fictitious points for later examination performances that were not delivered at all.
- Merely skipping an examination on the basis of an indefensible initial assumption justifies no points for the omitted performance.
- Fill in "folgefehlerhinweis" only if a special dependency or grading rule exists for the concrete step. Otherwise use null.

11. ASPECTS NOT TO BE GRADED

- Award no points of their own for:
  a) style,
  b) language,
  c) orthography,
  d) outward structure,
  e) headings,
  f) Gutachtenstil (the appraisal style) as such,
  g) mere length of the submission, or
  h) mere completeness without substantive quality.

- These aspects must not covertly enter the substantive grading guidance either.
- Formal presentation may be taken into account only insofar as it is itself a direct component of an expressly required legal examination performance.

12. FINAL CHECK

Check internally before output:

- Does every regular step and every alternative step carry an integer relative weight that is plausible and justified in relation to the remaining steps?
- Are all IDs globally unique and do they follow the naming scheme?
- Are all ID references valid (schwerpunkt_id, ersetzt_schritt_ids, nicht_kumulativ_mit, abhaengig_von)?
- Is every step assigned to at most one focal problem, and does every declared focal problem have at least one assigned step?
- Is a reference given for every explicit or implicit content?
- Is a concrete professional justification given for every externally supplemented content?
- Are the partial-credit levels per step ordered (weakest first) and cumulatively increasing?
- Is no student performance scored twice?
- Are the task instruction and the candidate instructions fully taken into account?
- Are far-fetched or merely theoretically conceivable alternatives excluded?
- Does the output contain no invented sources or claims of authority?
